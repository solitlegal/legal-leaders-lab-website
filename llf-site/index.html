Our approach

We solve your problem.

Every engagement starts with clarity and ends with ownership. Fixed scope where we can, clear deliverables always, and no open-ended retainers.

01

Assess

We map your legal function: workload, requests, tools, people and ambitions. You receive a roadmap with priorities and quick wins.

2 to 4 weeks · Fixed price
02

Design

We translate the roadmap into a target model: intake, workflows, roles, legal OKRs and the policies that hold it together.

Project scope
03

Build

We set up the front door, automate the workflows and build the AI skills and tools, side by side with your team.

Project price
04

Hand over

Your team owns everything we build: documentation, training and tools running on your infrastructure. No lock-in.
